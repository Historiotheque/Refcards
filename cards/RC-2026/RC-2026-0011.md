The method: document the work's procedure so thoroughly that the gaps are *mapped*
rather than unconscious. The 1907–1914 operation's gaps are unmapped (nothing was
logged); a contemporary operation running the New Documentation has gaps that are
known, bounded, and shrinking.

Compare: Picasso — object-centric documentation (dated works, kept everything, no
procedural records); Warhol — Time Capsules (610 boxes from 1974), daily diaries
(1976–87), Polaroid Big Shot exposures: more systematic, still surfaces/traces
rather than procedures. Nobody has done the whole nine yards: logging +
documenting + archiving the operation's *procedures* as standing methodology rather
than as the artwork itself.
