# Delta-workspace (ΔW)

The appreciable change between two successive configurations of the workspace:
if M(A) → A′ is the mutation carrying work-state A to work-state A′, the
delta-workspace is the difference Δ = A′ − A.

A delta is "appreciable" when the operator can sense it on entering the
workspace — a rearranged surface, a new instrument, a cleared desk. Progress in
the operation is measured in deltas, not in completed goals: what changed is
the record of work done.
