How documentable were art operations across history — from the Renaissance guilds'
contracts and account books, through Picasso's dated objects and Warhol's Time
Capsules, to the present — and what does that history reveal about what the New
Documentation must do for art operators today?

The curve is not monotonic: guilds (administrative traceability) > modern genius
(mystique, procedural collapse) > Warhol (partial recovery) > the Git era.
"Upping the documentation game" is a *recovery* of what the guilds had plus
something nobody ever had: procedural traceability. Track the documentation
*technologies* per era (contract, account book, dated canvas, Polaroid, tape,
diary-box, version control) alongside the granularity each permitted.
