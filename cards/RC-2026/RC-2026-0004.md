# Historiotopic velocity

The rate of change of the Historiotopia with respect to time — the derivative of
the historiotopic function h(t). The key operational metric of the workspace.

Low velocity: gentle deformation — continuity, the surface evolving without
breaking. High velocity: rupture — a tear in the surface, collapse, the deliberate
shutdown that resets the operation. The operator reads velocity the way a sailor
reads weather: to know whether to press on or take in sail.
