Why is the 1907–1914 collaboration's workspace/workflow so much less documentable
than the contemporary institutional production of Picasso/Cubism knowledge — and what
does that asymmetry reveal about what each operation records?

Operation A (Picasso–Braque, 1907–1914): documented works, not procedures.
Operation B (Picasso Administration, archives, writings, Succession/rights regime):
documents everything *about* the works. Operation B is the lens, not the object —
image rights constrain what its materials can carry in the paper.
