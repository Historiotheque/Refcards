The analytical grid for studying art operations as operations. Four clusters:

I. Core — Telos, Agents, Site. II. Program — Rhythms, Procedures, Experiments,
Materials & tools. III. Metabolism — Outputs & circulation, Economy, Social
infrastructure. IV. Memory — Documentation, Governance & decision points, Identity.

Centrality is dimension-specific (polycentricity): Picasso/Braque hub Experiments,
Kahnweiler hub Economy, Apollinaire hub Discourse. Flagged for schema v0.2.
